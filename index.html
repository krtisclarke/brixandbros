How to Play

Goal: stop the vacuums from reaching the Home Build. Each leak costs lives — bigger vacuums cost more. Survive every wave to win the level and unlock the next.

Difficulty: pick it when you start a battle. Easy — 30 waves, towers 15% cheaper, +25% lives. Medium — 40 waves, standard prices and lives. Hard — 50 waves, towers 15% pricier, −20% lives. Every campaign ends on a boss.

Unlocking: each difficulty has its own campaign. You advance one battlefield at a time, and clearing a whole tier of ten on a difficulty opens the next tier on that difficulty. Winning on a harder setting also counts for the easier ones — beat it on Hard and the Easy and Medium unlocks come free.

Second Chance: if the workshop falls, you can spend bricks to retry (Easy 25 · Medium 50 · Hard 100): lives fully restored, the field is cleared, and the wave that beat you replays — towers and studs are kept.

Bricks & boosts: winning a battle earns 🧱 bricks (Easy 100 · Medium 250 · Hard 500). Spend them in the Boost Shop (main menu) before a match: studs drops, spike walls, frenzy, a field-wide freeze, extra lives, an avalanche. Boosts you own sit in the command dock during battle — one click fires them.

Build: click a Bro in the bottom bar — or press its key. The bar is laid out like your keyboard: 1–5 Knights, Q–T Space, A–G Wizards, Z–B Crew. Then click the map to place (hold Shift to place several). Rovers and skiffs need a pool.

Manage: click a placed Bro — its card appears in the bottom-left. Q/W/E buy its three upgrade paths, T cycles targeting, X sells.

Three paths, choose two. Studs may go into only two of a Bro's three paths — buying into a second shuts the third for the battle. One of your two can reach its capstone (tier 3); the other stops at tier 2. Tiers 1 and 2 sharpen the numbers; every capstone changes how the Bro plays.

Vacuums: bigger ones split into smaller ones when destroyed. Watch for Stealth (needs detection), Armored (shred or pierce it), Regenerators, and bosses on waves 20, 30, 40, 50. The next wave's composition is previewed next to the Send button.

Economy: two currencies. 🔩 Studs is match money — pops and wave clears pay it, and it recruits and upgrades Bros (it resets each battle). 🧱 Bricks persist forever and buy boosts and retries. Parts Traders net extra studs every wave — early economy compounds fast.

Saving: autosaves after every wave; Ctrl/⌘+S (or the save button) saves mid-wave. Resume from the level screen. Progress lives in this browser — 💾 Back Up Progress (main menu) writes it all to one file you keep (on iPhone/iPad: Save to Files — an iCloud Drive folder syncs it to your other devices), and 📂 Load Backup restores it anywhere.
